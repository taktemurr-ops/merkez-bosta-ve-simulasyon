Idle & Simulation brings 15 calm simulations into one app. Idle production in the lantern workshop, crystal mine and toy factory; pottery merging; a farm sown by the seasons, a dream aquarium, a zen garden and an ant nest; town building and your cloud friend Pofu; a corner diner, a kitchen and a style studio. Seasons turn, calm mode never rushes you, and photo mode lets you decorate everything. Every game keeps its own progress, missions and achievements.

• Idle workshops: Lantern Workshop, Pottery Studio, Crystal Mine, Toy Factory
• Nature and calm: Seasons Farm, Dream Aquarium, Zen Garden, Ant Nest, Beehive Meadow
• Life and town: Pofu’s Home, Tiny Town
• Kitchen and style: Corner Diner, Kitchen Steps, Tea Garden, Style Studio

Highlights
• 100,000 levels per game; a bot plays every level to check its goal can be reached.
• Ten difficulty steps: relaxed early levels, expert challenges later.
• A turning year of seasons, a calm mode, photo and decorate mode, and earnings while you’re away.
• Daily and weekly levels, missions, achievements and 33 languages.
• Plays offline on phones, tablets and the web.
• No gambling, betting or 18+ content; suitable for all ages.

Purchases (such as removing ads) apply to every game in the app.
